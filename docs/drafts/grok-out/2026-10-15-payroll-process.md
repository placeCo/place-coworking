# PLACE Payroll Process (draft) — Oct 2026

ASSUMPTION: process only. No real names, accounts or photos. Verify with accountant.

## Monthly calendar
- Pay date: fix in contract (at least once per month).
- SSO (5% + 5%, max 875 each): remit by 15th of next month.
- PND1 (withholding tax): by 7th (or 15th if online) of next month.
- WCF: annual estimate + year-end reconcile.
- EWF (from 01.10.2026, 0.25% each side, no cap): only if 10+ staff and no qualifying provident fund. Due by 15th next month.

## Who does what
- Accounting: calculate, withhold, remit SSO/PND1/WCF/EWF, keep records.
- Ops: collect days/hours/advances/unpaid leave, confirm headcount.
- George: approve final paid amounts and any exceptions.
