Names and contacts filled only by George / Ops.

| Marketing / SMM | TG | EN/RU | content briefs | staff personal data |

## 15-min onboarding checklist
1. Confirm contact and language.
2. Show what the bot will send (3 examples).
3. Agree on report format (3 lines).
4. Note days off next 2 months.
5. Confirm what not to share with the bot.
