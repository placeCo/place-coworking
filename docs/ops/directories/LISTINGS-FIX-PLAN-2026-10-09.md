## 1. Brief rules for any listing (short)
- Hours: every day **08:00–23:00**. Never write 24/7 / open 24h / night access, not even as "not 24/7".
- Only three products: **Office** · **Place Pass (floors 1 and 3)** · **Podcast (one room, not a floor)**. Café, yoga, events, photo zone/photo studio, visa, condo are not the offer. Meeting rooms, terrace, rooftop, library, printing, kitchen are not products either: leave them out of texts and products.
- Place Pass: 50 ฿/hour · day **400 ฿ until 31.10.2026, 500 ฿ from 01.11.2026** · 1 800 ฿/week · 6 000 ฿/month · 15 000 ฿/3 months. Never call it cheap / best value / lowest price; don't compare with café-coworkings.
- Office: **30 000 ฿/month** (no company address) or **20 000 ฿/month on a 12-month contract**; the company registration address comes **only with the 12-month contract**. Floors **5 and 2**. Price is per office, not per person. Never "5 offices on floor 4". Never confirm availability in text: "Message us on WhatsApp and we'll show you what's available."
- Podcast: one room, no price in text: "Booking on WhatsApp +66 95 117 0481". Light and camera may be mentioned as rental with the podcast room, no price. Not floor 4, not a photo studio.
- **No free day on any listing** (no "1 free day", no "first trial day free", no free-day-pass product/toggle). The free day lives only in ads and flyers.
- Banned: 24/7 · 3 free days · 250 / 300 / 3 000 / 3 500 · cheapest / best value · Kathu · 83000 · "592" (write **59/2**) · Bangkok · visa / condo · five offices on floor 4.
- NAP: **59/2 Chao Fah Tawan Tok Rd, Chalong, Mueang Phuket 83130** · phone/WhatsApp **+66 95 117 0481** · **info@placecoworking.com** · placecoworking.com.
- From **01.11.2026** texts say **500 ฿/day only** (no 400). Structured day-price fields 400 → 500 on 01.11 (done by a person).
