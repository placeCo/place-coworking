## English

**Where are you?**  
Chalong, Phuket — 59/2 Chao Fah Tawan Tok Rd, Chalong, Mueang Phuket District, Phuket 83130. Not Kathu. Tel +66951170481 · info@placecoworking.com

**What are your hours?**  
Open daily 08:00–23:00. Office tenants have night access to their own office. Guests at night only on request via WhatsApp.

**Can I try for free?**  
There is no free day by default. A free first day applies only if you come from our ad/flyer or have code GOOGLEDAY (Place Pass, first visit only).

**Which floor do I work on?**  
Place Pass is one entry to floors 1 and 3. You work in the open space on floor 3. Floor 1 is the bonus zone: library, glassed café area, and meeting rooms (including large ~20-person setups) — not a separate desk product. Floor 2 is a leased private office — not for sale. Floor 4 is studio via manager. Floor 5 is private offices. The rooftop is leased — not coworking.

**What are the prices?**  
• 1 hour — 50฿ (short call / quick stop)  
• 1 day (10h) — 400฿ until 31 Oct 2026 (from 1 Nov — 500฿)  
• 1 week — 1,800฿  
• 1 month (08:00–23:00) — 6,000฿  
• 3 months — 15,000฿  
• Office floor 5 — 30,000฿/mo or 20,000฿/mo with a yearly deal  
• Meeting room — 250฿/h (−20% for Place Pass residents)  
• Studio floor 4 — ask the manager (no self-booking menu on the site)

**I saw older / cheaper prices online — are they valid?**  
No. Those public offers are removed. Short drop-in without a longer pass is day 400฿ (until 31 Oct; from 1 Nov — 500฿); better value is week 1,800฿ or month 6,000฿.

**Can I work at night?**  
Place Pass hours are 08:00–23:00 daily. Office tenants have night access to their own office.

**Can I sit on floor 2?**  
No. It is a closed leased office.

**Studio / filming?**  
Through the manager. There is no public “filming 500฿/hour” rate.

**Parking?**  
Across the street or alongside the building.

**Do you have private offices?**  
Yes — always. Floor **5**: private offices (up to 6 people) — 30,000฿/month or 20,000฿/month on a yearly deal. Floor **2**: office space we lease as offices (not a public hot-desk price list). Rare upgrades from Pass to floor 2 only if space is free — ask us. Tour via WhatsApp / +66951170481.

**Food / café?**  
Café is an extra, not the main product. Coworking offer is the Pass ladder above.
