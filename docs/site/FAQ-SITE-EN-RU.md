Public Place Pass hours: 08:00–23:00. We are not open 24/7 by default. Overnight (22:00–08:00) is only with the Month 24/7 package (9,000฿ + key deposit).

**Can I try for free?**  
Yes — one free trial day on the 3rd-floor open space only. Not three days. Not any floor. Studio and offices are not included.

Place Pass is one entry to floors 1 and 3. You work in the open space on floor 3 (trial is there too). Floor 4 is studio via manager. Floor 5 is private offices. The rooftop is leased — not coworking.

No. Those public offers are removed. Short drop-in without a longer pass is day 400฿ (until 31 Oct; from 1 Nov — 500฿); better value is week 1,800฿ or month 6,000฿.

Only on Month 24/7 (9,000฿). Otherwise until 23:00.
