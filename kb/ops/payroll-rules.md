# Payroll rules (from Aiz, 29.09.2026)

- SSO = 5% of salary AFTER unpaid-leave deduction (cap 875). Example: 17000 − 567 = 16433 × 5% = 821.65 ≈ 822.
- Unpaid leave: one day = monthly salary / 30 (17000/30 ≈ 567).
- Paid = Salary − Advance − SSO − Unpaid leave.
- Accountant checks the sheet. Accountant period: 21st–20th (to be confirmed).
- Salary sheets with bank accounts are NOT stored in the repo.
